Event Optimization

Maximize your rewards by aligning actions with event windows. Never waste resources outside their matching theme day.

⚡ Golden Hour

When Alliance Duel and Full Preparedness themes align at the same time, every action scores points in BOTH events simultaneously. Same resources, double rewards.

Alliance Duel Theme + Full Prep Theme = 2x Value

The Core Rule

Save all items throughout the week and deploy ONLY on the matching day. Items used outside their matching theme day score zero Alliance Duel points. This single rule separates efficient players from wasteful ones.

Speed-Up Rules
⏱️
Never use speed-ups randomly. Always align with the matching Alliance Duel day. Construction speed-ups → Day 2. Research speed-ups → Day 3. Training speed-ups → Day 5.
⚡
Golden Hour doubles everything. When Full Prep theme aligns with Duel theme (typically at 08:00 and 20:00 Apocalypse Time), deploy your biggest speed-ups here for 2x rewards.
📊
150% construction buff is the sweet spot. Diminishing returns kick in after this. Don't chase higher buffs — they're inefficient.
🏗️
Save for big upgrades. HQ, Laboratory, and Troop Camps benefit most from speed-ups. Don't waste them on low-level buildings.
Weekly Save Schedule
🔧 Day 1 — Vehicle: Save all golden wrenches, blueprints, and chip chests throughout the week. Open everything on this day only.
🏗️ Day 2 — Building: Stockpile construction speed-ups and orange refugees. Queue your biggest building upgrades to complete today.
🔬 Day 3 — Research: Use research speed-ups and badges. Start high-cost research mid-week so it finishes today. Prioritize orange trade trucks.
🦸 Day 4 — Heroes: Use hero fragments, cores, and skill books. Save Club recruitment tickets for this day.
🪖 Day 5 — Training: Mass troop production. Use all training speed-ups. Queue as many troop batches as possible.
💥 Day 6 — Combat: Kill enemies, complete bounty missions, attack rival bases. Stack all combat actions here.
