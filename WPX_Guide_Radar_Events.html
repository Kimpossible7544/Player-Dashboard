Core Principle: Never Explore Outside Events

Every radar exploration you do outside of an active radar event is wasted points. The items don't expire — so save them all until a radar event starts, then burn through your entire stockpile for maximum event points and milestone rewards.

Stacking Strategy
1

Collect Daily — Never Spend

Every day you receive free radar signals from daily rewards, alliance gifts, and login bonuses. Collect them all but do NOT use them. Let them pile up in your inventory between radar events.

Stockpile for 1-2 weeks between events
2

Track The Event Schedule

Radar events typically run on a regular cycle. Pay attention to in-game announcements, alliance chat, and event previews. Knowing when the next radar event hits lets you time your stacking perfectly.

Check event calendar daily for previews
3

Burn All At Once When Event Starts

The moment a radar event goes live, use ALL your saved signals and explorations. Each exploration gives event points. A big stockpile can push you through multiple milestone thresholds in one burst.

Dump entire stockpile on Day 1 of event
4

Use Refresh Tokens Strategically

If the radar board has low-value tiles, use a refresh token to reset it before exploring. Save your refreshes for event time too — getting better tiles during the event maximizes both event points and tile rewards.

Refresh for better tiles, then explore
5

Buy Extra Signals If Close To A Milestone

If you're a few explorations short of hitting the next milestone reward at the end of the event, it may be worth buying a few signals from the shop. The milestone rewards (hero shards, orange gear) are typically worth far more than the cost.

Only buy if milestone reward justifies cost

Pro Tips
⏰ Don't Forget Free Explorations: You get free daily explorations that reset every day. During a radar event, make sure to use these every single day before the timer resets. Outside events, these are the one exception — if they'll disappear unused, still avoid using them (they won't carry over).
🎯 Target Corner & Edge Tiles: Premium tiles with better rewards are often placed in corners or edges of the radar board. Use refresh tokens if your board is filled with low-value center tiles.
📊 Count Before You Spend: Before the event, count your total signals and check how many points each exploration gives. Calculate which milestone you can realistically hit and plan accordingly — don't buy extras if you can't reach the next threshold.
🤝 Alliance Coordination: Some radar events have alliance-wide point totals. Coordinate with your alliance so everyone dumps their stockpile at the same time to maximize alliance milestone rewards.
💰 Alliance Shop Priority: Radar signals available in the alliance shop are some of the best value purchases. Buy them weekly during the save phase so you have a larger stockpile when the event arrives.
🚫 Ignore The Red Notification Dot: Your radar icon will show a notification badge when you have unused signals. Train yourself to ignore it. That red dot is trying to trick you into wasting points outside of events.
